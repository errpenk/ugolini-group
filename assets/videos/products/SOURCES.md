# Product detail films

The 86 current product pages use pairs of short, silent Pexels films. Each product's pair and each film's original Pexels page are listed in [`data/product-films.json`](../../../data/product-films.json). Files were downloaded on 2026-10-29, converted to web-ready H.264 MP4, and given a still poster from the same film.

The [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/) permits commercial website and promotional use without attribution. The footage illustrates ingredients and serving ideas; it does not depict Ugolini products, staff, facilities, or their manufacturing process. Descriptive labels in the site should not imply otherwise.
